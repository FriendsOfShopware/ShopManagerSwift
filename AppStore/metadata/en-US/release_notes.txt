First release of Shopware Shop Manager.

- Connect and switch between multiple Shopware 6 stores
- Dashboard with today's revenue, targets, and a needs-attention list
- Orders: search, filter, full detail, state transitions, documents, tracking
- Customers, Products (with quick edits), Promotions, Reviews, and Media
- Reports with sales-over-time and breakdowns by channel, country, and more
- Home/lock-screen widgets and optional new-order push notifications
- Native layout for iPhone, iPad, and Mac
